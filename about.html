About Fapong.gallery

A tribute to Jaipong dance, a cultural treasure that embodies grace, joy, and the spirit of West Java.

Fapong.gallery was created to preserve and share the beauty of Jaipong dance, bringing its timeless elegance to a wider audience, both in Indonesia and beyond.

Through our collection of performances, stories, and cultural insights, we aim to keep this vibrant tradition alive for future generations.
